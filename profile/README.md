# Showcase Remodels

**Veteran owned. Employee installers. Lifetime warranty.**

Showcase Remodels has been transforming South Jersey bathrooms for more than 24 years. Founded by U.S. Army veteran Sam DeMaio out of Turnersville / Sewell, NJ, we specialize in tub to shower conversions done in as little as a day, built for homeowners 55+.

## What makes us different

* **Our own people, every install.** No subcontractors. Every installer is a Showcase employee.
* **Lifetime warranty.** We stand behind the work for as long as you own the home.
* **Done in as little as a day.** One Day Bath gets you a safer, better bathroom fast.

## Our divisions

| Division | What it does |
|---|---|
| Showcase Remodels | Full bathroom remodels across South Jersey |
| One Day Bath | Tub to shower conversions done in as little as a day |
| Signature Reflections | Luxury bathroom products and an authorized dealer network |
